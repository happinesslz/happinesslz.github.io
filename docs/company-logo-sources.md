# Industry collaboration logos

The homepage lists past collaborations supplied by Zhe Liu. Company names, links,
and card colors are maintained in `_data/industry_collaborations.yml`.

Logos were retrieved from the companies' official websites on 2026-09-28 and are
stored locally to avoid third-party image requests. The Huawei mark is the one
displayed by Huawei Noah's Ark Lab; the Yinwang mark identifies the former Huawei
Automotive BU under its current name.
